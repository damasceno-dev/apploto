# Reports and month lock — blueprint

> **Design M4 · D04 (2026-10-20), corrected six-report scope accepted in 4.8.5; three-report/IA extension (2026-10-22) awaiting independent acceptance in 4.8.4.** Feeds **Web/Mobile M8**. Semantics: catalog entries [Daily ledger](../../docs/screens.md#daily-ledger), [Fiado balance](../../docs/screens.md#fiado-balance), [Fiado aging](../../docs/screens.md#fiado-aging), [Open-cheque aging](../../docs/screens.md#open-cheque-aging), [Cash-variance summary](../../docs/screens.md#cash-variance-summary), [Monthly reconciliation + lock](../../docs/screens.md#monthly-reconciliation--lock). Contracts: server M7/M7.7 and M11 Phases 2–7, including the three cataloged M11 reports. **2026-10-22 · M4 4.8.4:** the source-contract gate is accepted at `84f75b9`; the three new blueprints and D-M2-1 integration below are authored and awaiting independent design acceptance. The six earlier blueprints retain their accepted D04 disposition; this extension does not reopen it.

Every report follows one shape so a manager learns it once: a header that names the report, a filter bar that names the scope, a summary strip of **whole-set** figures from the response, and a table whose rows open the record they summarize. Page-local sums are labelled as such, and no report adds money across accounts into a figure the server did not return.

## Shared report frame

The family has nine Manager/Admin destinations: the six operational reports below plus Dashboard, Upcoming settlements and Monthly financial report. The six retain the common summary/table pattern; Dashboard composes source-specific bounded sections, Upcoming adds independent overdue totals, and Monthly financial keeps four distinct measures and their detailed breakdowns. The shell navigation and the route audit below cover all nine.

1. **PageHeader** — report title; context line with the applied scope in words.
2. **[FilterBar](../components/filter-bar.md)** — the endpoint's own query fields only; **Ver** applies; scope summary below.
3. **[SummaryStrip](../components/summary-strip.md)** — whole-set totals from the response, each with its period; signed values use `dom.cash-variance.*`, balances `dom.fiado-balance.*`.
4. **[Table](../components/table.md)** — rows ordered by the server; record destinations as explicit links; Pagination under it.
5. **Explanation** *(where the catalog has one)* — one quiet paragraph or a collapsed Disclosure (FIFO netting, what excludes Draft rows).

Responsive behavior for the six earlier reports: summary strip 4 → 2 → 1 columns; tables scroll inside their region with the first column sticky between `breakpoint.medium` and `breakpoint.wide`, and become grouped lists below `breakpoint.medium` (primary line: the row's identity; second line: its figures; trailing link). States for the six earlier reports: loading (Skeleton strip and rows), empty (scoped EmptyState), error (Notice with retry; filters kept), updating, success. Drill-downs record the report with its filters as origin; closing a report returns to Manager Work Queue when nothing narrower opened it.

## Daily ledger

- **Filters:** Conta (required; preselects the last account viewed on this device), De / Até (≤ 366 days; the server's 400 renders verbatim).
- **Summary:** Saldo inicial · Entradas · Saídas · Líquido · Saldo final — for the one account and window; an **Inativa** badge beside a deactivated account's name.
- **Table:** Data da transação · Tipo · Categoria · Cliente · Registrado por · Descrição · Direção · Valor · Vencimento · Pagamento · **Abrir transação**. Direction is text, never profit/loss color.
- **Notes:** the running balance is a ledger balance, not cash on hand; the header says **Movimento da conta**, never "caixa".

## Fiado balance

- **Filters:** Posição em (as-of date, default branch today), Cliente.
- **Tabs ([Tabs](../components/tabs.md)):** **Dívidas em aberto ({DebtorCount})** with **Total em aberto** = `TotalDebt` · **Saldo crédito ({CreditorCount})** with **Crédito dos clientes** = `TotalCredit` as a positive amount. The net `TotalOutstanding` is not shown as an amount to collect.
- **Table per tab:** Cliente · Saldo (mapped display) · actions **Ver extrato** (Client statement, tab and filters kept for return), **Ver por idade** (Fiado aging with the client carried), **Receber pagamento**.
- **Empty:** **Nenhum cliente com dívida em aberto.** / **Nenhum cliente com crédito.**

## Fiado aging

- **Filters:** Posição em, Cliente, Conta fiado.
- **Summary:** **Total em aberto** and one figure per bucket (`ByBucket`, zeros included) with the bucket badge (`dom.aging-bucket.*`) as the label, plus **Venda mais antiga em aberto**.
- **Explanation:** **Os recebimentos de cada cliente quitam primeiro as vendas mais antigas, em todas as contas fiado. Só o saldo restante de cada venda aparece aqui.**
- **Table:** Data da transação · Vencimento · Dias em aberto · Faixa (badge) · Cliente · Conta · Descrição · Valor restante · **Abrir transação** (read context; `PaidAt` disabled on fiado). Order: oldest exposure first (server).

## Open-cheque aging

- **Filters:** Posição em, Conta, Cliente.
- **Summary:** **Total em aberto**, **Lançamentos em aberto**, per-bucket figures; `TotalCount` is the number of groups (plans plus standalone cheques), labelled **Grupos em aberto**.
- **Table:** expandable plan groups ([Disclosure](../components/disclosure.md) per row): Cliente · Conta · Descrição · **{abertas}/{total} parcelas em aberto** · Vencimento mais antigo · Faixa · Total em aberto; expanded rows: Vencimento · Valor · Dias · Faixa · **Abrir parcela** (where `PaidAt` is set as cheques clear). A null `OriginTransactionId` identifies a **Cheque avulso** one-row group: show its transaction directly with **Abrir transação**, without inventing a plan or a plan-finalization action. Plans remain included independently of their type's instrument; standalone rows require `Cheque`, including retired classified types. Totals cover both, with paid, Draft and cancelled rows excluded.
- **Compact:** each plan is a block with its summary line and a **Ver parcelas** disclosure; each standalone cheque is a direct transaction block. Keep the report's account/client/as-of filters for return.

## Cash-variance summary

- **Filters:** De / Até (default: month to date), Conta.
- **Summary:** Total · Média · Maior · Menor (signed, colored by `dom.cash-variance.*` with **falta/sobra** captions) and **Enviados / Aprovados / Rejeitados** counts; caption **Rascunhos (inclusive desfeitos, reabertos e em correção) ficam fora.**
- **Table:** Data · Conta · Situação (badge) · Diferença · status-routed link (**Conferir** for Submitted; **Ver** otherwise). Each row opens by its own `DailyCloseId`.
- **Empty:** **Nenhuma diferença oficial neste período.** with the caption that a Draft close has no variance row yet.

## Monthly reconciliation + lock

### Regions

1. **PageHeader** — **Fechamento mensal**; month navigation (**Mês anterior** · `MM/yyyy` · **Próximo mês**); context: **Data de bloqueio {data}** or **Nunca bloqueado**; month state badges **Bloqueado** / **Em andamento** / **Encerrado, não bloqueado**.
2. **Earlier month to resolve** *(after a refused lock)* — warning Notice with the server's verbatim message and **Ir para {MM/yyyy}**, placed above everything so the month never has to be hunted.
3. **Prontidão** — a [Card](../components/card.md): **Pronto para bloquear** (success text) or **{n} bloqueios** (warning text), with the advisory note **A verificação final é feita no bloqueio.** and **Bloquear mês** (primary; absent for the current or a locked month, with the reason).
4. **Bloqueios** — list of structured blockers as sentences (copy §5 labels + day + account), each with its destination: status-routed close, **Ver rascunhos** (Transactions list, Draft filter, that day) or **Abrir o dia**.
5. **Dias do mês** — table: Dia · Fechamentos (account + status badge + official variance) · Ativas · Rascunhos · Canceladas · Diferença líquida; compact: one block per day with only non-empty facts.

### Lock flow

**Bloquear mês** opens a confirmation: **Bloquear {mês}?** — **Depois do bloqueio, transações, fechamentos e registros de ponto até {último dia} não podem mais ser alterados.** — **Bloquear mês** (primary) / **Voltar**. It sends the Setting `ETag`; a stale setting returns **Recarregar e conferir**; an unresolved earlier month shows region 2; coordination busy is retryable; success shows **Mês bloqueado** with the new lock date and the month in its locked state.

### States

Loading; clean month; blockers present; locked (read-only, no lock action); current month (lock action absent: **O mês atual não pode ser bloqueado.**); out-of-range month (verbatim 400); error; uncertain lock (warning Notice; re-read the report and settings before anything else).

The following three sections complete the existing nine-report family, using the [selected direction](../../docs/decisions.md), [token model](../../tokens/README.md) and [component contract](../components/README.md). C# PascalCase field names below correspond to generated camelCase JSON properties; apps consume generated types, never a copied DTO. The [catalog](../../docs/screens.md#dashboard) keeps field-level source attribution. All fields listed below are **[DTO]**; human-readable period, status, missing-value and return labels are **[composed]** from those fields, not new metrics.

All three reports require a live Manager/Admin branch session, including an unlinked Manager. Their URLs carry branch context per [D-M2-1](../../docs/decisions.md#d-m2-1--independent-web-branch-context), never permission. A Member sees no navigation link; a direct entry meets the session/role gate. Opening these reports enters Gestão; shared transaction routes keep the authorized originating mode. ### State presentation

- **Loading:** PageHeader, filter shell and clock labels remain; one busy results landmark with Skeleton sections. Do not stream successful widgets before the whole response succeeds.
- **Updating:** keep the last complete snapshot labelled **Atualizando…** and its applied month/clock. A newly staged month is not the displayed month until success. Disable duplicate refresh, keep navigation to the explicitly old snapshot scope available. Success replaces every region together, announces **Dashboard atualizado — {mês}; posição em {data}**, without moving focus.
- **Ready / Atualizado:** show real figures, including recorded zero, and bounded rows. This badge is response state, not a promise of live data. Never turn an idle account's zero into missing data.
- **Empty / Nada no período:** show a scoped sentence and the report link, not a fabricated zero KPI panel. Keep reconciliation context and legitimate other sections. An Empty monthly section still retains **Tarifa de bolão não configurada** / **Sobras não configuradas** for any missing marker, independent of its aggregate Status. Fiado credit-only is Ready, not overdue debt; A receber with overdue only is Ready, with **Nenhum vencimento neste intervalo** and the overdue line. Empty branch follows returned Empty/NotConfigured, not inferred array lengths.
- **NotConfigured / Não configurado:** only where returned (A receber and Financeiro). Explain the missing marker and offer **Configurar tipos**. Monthly official variance/hours remain visible even when both markers are missing; use **Tarifa de bolão não configurada** / **Sobras não configuradas**, never an ambiguous amount placeholder. Configuration return refreshes the whole snapshot; metadata changes can alter historical classifications.
- **Error:** atomic failure, cancellation, 400, 401, 403 or 5xx never exposes whichever sections completed. Replace results with Notice, retain filters and show every `ErrorMessages` verbatim. **Tentar novamente** repeats only the GET. If an update failed, the prior snapshot may be explicitly offered as **Dados anteriores — desatualizados** in its entirety, with its old clock/scope, never mixed into a fresh success. 401 uses identity recovery; 403 rechecks membership and role without renewal loops. No 404 record lookup is part of this endpoint.

### Responsive and accessible behavior

At desktop, section headings and scope always precede aligned numbers. At tablet use one column if either table crowds; each preview has its own labelled horizontal scroll region and sticky action column, never page-wide overflow. At 375px use grouped rows in the exact order above. Within each row put identity/date, status, principal amount, then explicitly labelled details and trailing link; recorder/submitter and generated shares wrap beneath, never disappear. The eleven headings form a navigable heading outline; optional contents links jump to them without hiding regions.

Focus: shell skip link → header return → month → Ver → Atualizar → optional section anchors → each region's full-report link and row links in reading order. No whole-row extra tab stop; nested plan/report links stop row navigation. Link names include action, account/client/operator, date/status and amount as relevant, e.g. **Ver fechamento do Terminal 2, 20/10, enviado**. Enter activates; keyboard back returns to the saved row/section; missing row returns focus to section heading. Filters announce one atomic result, not eleven simultaneous live regions. On error focus the Notice heading only after a user-requested failed read; initial gating focuses the destination explanation. Touch links meet existing 44px minimum / 48px comfortable target and gaps through component tokens. Large text wraps labels and provenance, keeps full signed numbers without ellipsis, and stacks sections earlier. Light/dark convey state in text as well as color. No written specification is screen-reader or phone validation.

## Upcoming settlements

**Initial business date and midnight:** before the first fresh-entry query, obtain/refresh the shell’s `GET /branch/current` and use its calendar-only `BranchLocalDate` for the today→today+13 default; never use the device date. While this read is loading, keep default-dependent filters/results loading; on failure show retry and do not fabricate defaults. Once the report arrives, its `AsOfDate` is authoritative for overdue labels and displayed position. If it differs from the shell/default date (including midnight), keep the requested DueDateFrom/To and explicit user filters, display the returned window/AsOfDate, refresh shell context and announce **A data da filial mudou. Confira o período antes de continuar.** Offer **Usar período de hoje** to stage a fresh AsOfDate→+13 default, applied with **Ver**; never silently roll a selected window or re-label old data as current. Restored report filters remain explicit, not fresh-entry defaults.

### Header, filter and totals

PageHeader **A receber** with report origin; scope **Vencimentos de {DueDateFrom} a {DueDateTo} · posição em {AsOfDate}**. FilterBar (apply with **Ver**): **De / Até** due dates, **Conta** (all by default; may include deactivated branch accounts), **Recebível** (all, Cheque, Cartão de crédito, Cartão de débito). First fresh entry uses branch today through +13 inclusive, page 1, page size 50. This is a UI default; request dates/Page/PageSize are required and the DTO does not supply these defaults. Page size control offers 25/50/100/200; backend permits 1–200, Page ≥1. Changing filter/size resets page 1; do not invent search, sort, client or status filters.

Call `GET /report/upcoming-settlements` with `DueDateFrom`, `DueDateTo`, optional `AccountId`, optional `Instrument`, `Page`, `PageSize`. `None` is not a valid instrument filter; **Todos** omits it. Required, ordered date window uses the server's exact cap `(DueDateTo − DueDateFrom).TotalDays ≤ 366` (inclusive endpoints; do not add a stricter client rule). Future due dates are allowed; no future-month rejection belongs to this endpoint.

SummaryStrip maps `TotalCount` **Lançamentos no intervalo**, `TotalValue` **A receber no intervalo**, `ByInstrument` with each `Instrument`, `Count`, `Value` in declared Cheque/CreditCard/DebitCard order, including zeros when configured. A separate ruled line **Vencidos antes de {AsOfDate}: {OverdueCount} · {OverdueValue}** uses the same account/instrument filters across all dates before today. Never add it to window money: windows starting in the past already include overlapping overdue rows. `IsConfigured` is branch-wide, not a claim that the selected instrument has rows.

### Rows, source meaning and drill-down

Table follows `DueDate, Date, Id` server order. Priority columns: **Vencimento · Valor · Recebível · Conta · Tipo de transação · Cliente · Data da transação · Abrir**. `TransactionId` is row identity; `OriginTransactionId?` identifies its plan. Render `Instrument` through `dom.settlement-instrument.*` and copy §5; `TransactionTypeId/Name`, `AccountId/Name`, `ClientId/Name?` preserve identity with verbatim names. Missing optional client is **Sem cliente**. No activity flags or transaction status/PaidAt are returned in this item: do not infer **Tipo desativado** or **Conta inativa** from names or missing picker options. Explicit flags from a separately authorized entity read may label inactivity; otherwise selected unavailable options use **fora das opções atuais**. Never require an active-only picker to discard a valid inactive account filter.

Rows are Active and unpaid under server predicates (`Active`, `Status=Active`, `PaidAt=null`, current type instrument ≠None); Draft/cancelled/paid rows are excluded. Show **A receber**, with **Vencido** only if DueDate < returned AsOfDate (equality means due today). These are composed meanings, not invented status fields. An installment appears once if its current type is classified; this differs from open-cheque aging, which includes plan rows independently of the instrument marker. A retired classified type remains included. `SettlementInstrument` describes the receivable, `SettlementRule` timing and `MonthlyFinancialRole` financial classification remain independent.

### Financial meaning and working assumptions

Show a concise note beside the four figures: **Valores distintos; não somam entre si. Comparação com o borderô da CEF feita manualmente.** Official variance reads persisted non-Draft Submitted/Approved/Rejected closes, each once; Draft retained values are suppressed. It is neither all approved-only variance nor an estimate from transaction movement. The same variance appears in other reports; do not add it again.

`MonthlyFinancialRole.BolaoServiceFee` identifies the fee, `CommercialLeftover` identifies loss; never match names, product labels, SettlementRule or SettlementInstrument. Role rows are Active transactions on Terminal accounts in the report window; Draft/cancelled and other account types stay out. The backend returns fee net In−Out and leftover loss Out−In; these formulas explain source meaning only, apps display the returned figures. Own fee revenue is not gross account movement, acquirer settlement or cash on hand; hours are separate units. A zero configured fee/loss is real zero; negative net figures retain their sign under neutral **Valor líquido da tarifa** / **Valor líquido de sobras** labels; the signed aggregate alone does not prove a reversal, refund or profit.

Place **Definição provisória — aguarda confirmação da lotérica** beside fee/leftovers. Expanded explanation: M9.5 working Q3 (canonical §9 Q4/SQ3) treats the actual day's fee as own revenue recorded on Terminal and summed for the month; working Q4 (§9 Q5/SQ4) treats unsold Bolão quotas/Federal fractions as commercial losses. SQ5 (§9 Q6, adopted owner attribution) keeps closes per Terminal/day and hours per operator/day. Q9 treats **Operador** as a typed expected In movement; its final shop meaning/label remains unresolved. It is not an extra monthly revenue figure or automated fee. Q3/Q9 are prospective working assumptions, not verified shop practice, historical Access parity or first-transition reconciliation; M9.5/AA/M10 retain their gates.

A Disclosure **Sobre as classificações** explains: **Os relatórios usam a classificação atual dos tipos. Alterar uma classificação pode mudar a leitura de meses anteriores, inclusive bloqueados; não altera os lançamentos, a diferença oficial nem o bloqueio.** **Configurar tipos** opens Categories & Transaction Types and returns to the same month. Existing unclassified types give missing figures, retired classified types still count. Classification is metadata, never a new financial rule in this screen.

Client credit is not a monthly report field. Do not import a fiado balance into these totals or transform positive displayed credit into overdue debt. If a nested ledger opens a client statement, keep the statement's positive credit presentation and source chain; it contributes no additional monthly KPI.
